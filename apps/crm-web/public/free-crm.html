A free CRM for small teams

For teams that are still tracking customers in a spreadsheet because every CRM they tried wanted a card first.

BrokeCRM is a customer relationship manager you can start using in about a minute. Sign in with your email, Google or GitHub, invite the people you work with, and you all see the same contacts, companies and deals. There is nothing to buy and no trial that runs out.

What a small team gets

  • A deal pipeline. Deals are cards on a board. Drag one from Opportunity to Won as it moves, and rename the stages to match how you sell.
  • Contacts and companies. Each person has one page with their details, their company, every note and every task.
  • Notes with files. Write down what was said after a call and attach the proposal you sent.
  • Tasks. Give a follow-up a date and see what is due today and what is overdue.
  • Your whole team. Everyone works in one shared organization. Seats are not priced.
  • Import, export and search. Bring contacts in from a CSV file, take them out again whenever you want, and search across everything.

What "free" means here

The free plan holds 500 contacts per organization today. There is no card to enter, no countdown and no feature that unlocks when you pay, because there is nothing to pay for. BrokeCRM is hosted for you, so there is also nothing to install or keep running.

It stays honest because it is open: all of BrokeCRM is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. If the hosted version ever stops suiting you, export your data or run your own copy.

Moving off a spreadsheet

Export your sheet as a CSV file, import it on the Contacts page, and your people and their companies are in. From there, add a deal for each conversation that could turn into money and a task for each thing you promised to do. That is the whole setup.

If you use an AI assistant, it can do the data entry for you: BrokeCRM has an MCP server that lets Claude and other assistants read and update your CRM as you.

Questions people ask

Is BrokeCRM really free?

Yes. There is nothing to buy and no card to enter. The free plan holds 500 contacts per organization today.

How many people can use it?

Your whole team can work in one organization. BrokeCRM does not charge per seat.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Contacts, companies and deals export to CSV from the app, and everything is also available through the public API.

Do I need to install anything?

No. BrokeCRM is hosted for you at brokecrm.cloud. You sign in and use it in the browser, on a computer or a phone.

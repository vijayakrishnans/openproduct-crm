Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

The short version. We keep the email address you sign in with and the data you put into your CRM, and we use them only to run the service. We do not sell data, show ads, or track you: there are no analytics and no tracking cookies. The code that handles your data is open source, so you can check.

1. Who is responsible

BrokeCRM is the hosted service at www.brokecrm.cloud, operated by the maintainers of vijayakrishnans/openproduct-crm ("we"). This policy covers that hosted service. If you run your own copy of the software, you are responsible for the data in it.

There are two kinds of personal data here, and our role differs. For your account, we decide how the data is used. For the people you record in your CRM (your contacts), you or your organization decide; we only store and process that data for you.

2. What we collect, and why

DataWhy we have it
Your email address, and a name if you give oneTo create your account, sign you in, and send sign-in codes and invitations
If you sign in with Google or GitHub: your email address, display name and that provider's id for youTo sign you in. We do not receive your password and do not read anything else from that account
What you put in the CRM: contacts, companies, deals, notes, tasks, tags and attached filesTo provide the CRM to you and your organization
Your organization, its members, their roles, invitations and API keysTo control who can see and change what
Sign-in and security events, with IP address and browser typeTo protect accounts and investigate misuse
A record of changes in your organization (the audit log)So your organization can see who did what
Technical request logs kept by our hosting providerTo operate and troubleshoot the service

We do not collect payment details: the service is free and nothing is sold on it.

3. What we do not do

  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and no tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not use your CRM data to train AI models.
  • We do not read your CRM data except where needed to fix a problem you report or to keep the service secure.

4. Cookies and browser storage

  • Your sign-in is kept in your browser's local storage on this site, so you stay signed in. Signing out removes it.
  • Your display preferences (such as the theme) are kept the same way.
  • If you sign in with Google or GitHub, one short-lived cookie is set during the sign-in itself to protect it against forgery. It expires within minutes.
  • The demo (/?demo) runs entirely in your browser on made-up data. Nothing you type there is sent to us.

5. Who else handles the data

We use a small number of providers to run the service. They process data on our behalf.

ProviderWhat for
CloudflareHosting of the app and API, file storage for attachments, and sending sign-in emails
SupabaseThe database, hosted in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1)
Google, GitHubOnly if you choose to sign in with them

Two features send limited data elsewhere at your direction. To show a logo, the service fetches the public favicon of a company's website or of a contact's email domain. And if you connect an AI assistant through the MCP server or use an API key, that tool receives the CRM data you let it read.

We disclose personal data to others only if the law requires it.

6. Where data is stored

The database is in Singapore. Cloudflare serves the app from its network around the world, so requests may be handled in the country you connect from. If you are in a region with rules on international transfers, using the service means your data is stored outside it.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and CRM data are kept while your account or organization exists.
  • When you delete a record in the CRM it is removed, and files attached to a deleted note are deleted with it.
  • Sign-in codes expire within minutes. Security events and the audit log are kept to protect accounts and for your organization's own record.
  • When you ask us to delete an account or an organization, we delete its data within 30 days, apart from what we must keep by law.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can see and correct your profile in the app, and export your contacts, companies and deals.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your personal data, or to correct or delete it, or to delete your account or organization. The app does not yet have a button for deleting an organization, so this goes through us (section 11).
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights, including to object to processing and to complain to your data protection authority.
  • If you are a person recorded in someone's BrokeCRM, please contact the organization that recorded you; it decides what happens to that data. If you cannot reach it, tell us and we will pass the request on.

9. Security

Each organization's data is kept apart from every other's, in the database and in the API. Connections are encrypted. The code is public, and security reports are welcome through the project's security policy. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you without undue delay.

10. Children

BrokeCRM is a tool for work and is not meant for children. You must be at least 16 to have an account.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced on this site.

Privacy questions and requests: open an issue on GitHub. Please do not put personal data in a public issue; say that you have a private request and we will arrange a private channel.

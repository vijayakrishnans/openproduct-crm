The CRM for teams with no CRM budget.

BrokeCRM keeps your contacts, companies, deals, notes and tasks in one place, for your whole team. It is free, the code is open, and we host it for you.

Start free Try the demo

No card, no trial clock. Sign in with your email and you are in. The demo runs in your browser on made-up data.

What you get

The parts of a CRM a small team uses every day, and nothing to configure first.

  • A deal pipeline

    Drag deals from stage to stage on a board. Rename the stages to match how you sell.

  • Contacts and companies

    Everyone you talk to, who they work for, and every note and deal attached to them.

  • Notes with files

    Write down what was said and attach the proposal. It stays with the contact or the deal.

  • Tasks

    Follow-ups with a due date, so the next call does not depend on anyone's memory.

  • Your whole team

    Invite colleagues to your organization. Each organization's data is kept apart from every other.

  • Import, export, search

    Bring contacts in from a CSV, take everything out again, and find anything from one search box.

  • Ready for AI agents

    An MCP server lets your AI assistant read and update the CRM with an API key you control.

  • An API and webhooks

    Everything the app does goes through a public API, and changes can notify your other tools.

Free, and open all the way down

BrokeCRM is open source under the Apache 2.0 licence. Use the hosted version here, or read the code and run your own.

Is it really free?
Yes. There is nothing to buy and no card to enter. The free plan holds 500 contacts per organization today.
Who hosts it?
We do, on Cloudflare, deployed with Orunbase. You sign in and use it; there is nothing to install.
Can I run my own copy?
Yes. The whole product is in one public repository, with the steps to deploy it.
Where does it come from?
The app is built on Atomic CRM by Marmelab, an open-source CRM under the MIT licence. BrokeCRM adds accounts for teams, hosting, an API and the MCP server. It is a separate product, not Atomic CRM and not a Marmelab product. It is listed as an open build on openproduct.
